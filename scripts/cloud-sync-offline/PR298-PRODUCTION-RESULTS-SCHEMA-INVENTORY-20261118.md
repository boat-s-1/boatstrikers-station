## Constraints, indexes, trigger

- `bs_race_results_pkey`: PRIMARY KEY (`id`).
- `bs_race_results_race_date_course_code_race_no_key`: UNIQUE (`race_date, course_code, race_no`).
- Three indexes: the two backing unique btree indexes above, and `idx_bs_results_race` on (`race_date, course_code, race_no`).
- One noninternal trigger: `trg_bs_track_result_first_seen` AFTER INSERT OR UPDATE FOR EACH ROW, calls `bs_track_result_first_seen()`.

## Security
